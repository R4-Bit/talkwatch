## What is TalkWatch?

TalkWatch is an always-on voice-activated clock and personal safety app that runs directly in your phone's browser — no app store required. It tells you the time in a British AI voice, keeps your screen on 24/7, and — most importantly — can call 911, announce your location to emergency responders, and identify you by name, all hands-free using just your voice.

Think of it as a smartwatch, a safety device, and a personal assistant — all in one screen.

---

## Features

### ⏰ Time — Free
- Clock always visible on screen — no unlocking needed
- Say **"TIME"** → British AI voice announces the time with a motivational quote, math fact, or funny one-liner
- Shows current date, day of week, and live seconds
- Screen stays on automatically (Wake Lock enabled)

### 📍 Live Location Display — Free
- Your current cross street and city displayed below the clock at all times
- Refreshes every 2 minutes automatically
- Uses GPS — no account needed

### 📱 Find My Phone — Free
- Say **"PHONE"** → your phone rings loudly and vibrates so you can find it
- Tap "Found it!" to dismiss

### 🚨 Emergency 911 — TalkWatch EMERGENT
- Say **"HELP"**, **"CALL 911"**, or **"OUCH"** → emergency sequence begins immediately
- AI voice announces: *"9-1-1 is being called. Connecting now. Do you wish to stay on the line?"*
- Your GPS location is spoken aloud automatically to the 911 responder
- Every 6 seconds a female AI voice repeats: *"This caller, [YOUR LAST NAME, FIRST NAME], is using TalkWatch"* — so responders always know who you are even if you can't speak
- Phone vibrates to alert people nearby

### 📍 Send My Location — TalkWatch EMERGENT
- Say **"LOCATION"** or **"MY LOCATION"** → opens your email with your exact GPS coordinates and a Google Maps link pre-filled, ready to send to anyone
- AI voice also speaks your coordinates aloud during a 911 call

### 🔴 End / Cancel
- Say **"STOP"** or **"HANG UP"** → cancels everything, silences the AI voice, closes the emergency screen

## Voice Commands — Quick Reference

| Say this | What happens |
|----------|-------------|
| **"TIME"** | Announces time + quote |
| **"PHONE"** | Rings & vibrates to find phone |
| **"HELP"** or **"CALL 911"** | Calls 911 + speaks your location |
| **"LOCATION"** or **"MY LOCATION"** | Emails your location + speaks it |
| **"STOP"** or **"HANG UP"** | Ends call / cancels everything |

## How to Install on YOUR Phone

### Step 1 — Open the app
On your phone browser go to:
```
https://yourusername.github.io/talkwatch.html
```

### Step 2 — Add to Home Screen

**On iPhone (Safari):**
1. Tap the **Share button** (box with arrow pointing up) at the bottom
2. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Tap **"Add"** in the top right
4. TalkWatch icon now lives on your home screen!

**On Android (Chrome):**
1. Tap the **three dots menu** (⋮) top right
2. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Tap **"Add"**
4. TalkWatch icon now lives on your home screen!

### Step 3 — Set screen to never sleep
- **iPhone:** Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → **Never**
- **Android:** Settings → Display → Screen Timeout → **Never**

### Step 4 — Allow permissions when asked
- **Location** → tap Allow (needed for 911 location feature)
- **Microphone** → tap Allow (needed for voice commands)

### Step 5 — Enter your name
- First time you open TalkWatch it asks for your full name
- Enter **First Last** (example: Roland Rosas)
- This is used to identify you to 911 responders automatically

## Tips for Best Use

- **Keep TalkWatch open** on your screen at all times — it works like a watch
- **Plug in your charger** if leaving it on overnight
- **Test it first** — say "TIME" to make sure the voice works before you need the safety features
- **Set your email** in your phone contacts as the default so the location email sends fast
- The **location below the clock** updates automatically — glance at it anytime to know your cross street

## Privacy

- Location is accessed **only when you use a feature** that needs it
